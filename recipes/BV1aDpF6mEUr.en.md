## 1 Low heat folded eggs with buttered baguette

Time reference: 00:00–01:03

**Ingredients**: Eggs, a little oil, baguette and softened butter, with cucumber and nuts alongside.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Reheat the baguette for 2 minutes. Temperature, egg count and egg cooking time are unspecified.

1. Spray a little oil into the pan, add beaten egg and lower the heat.
2. Gently push and stir so the inside gradually sets, then fold into a thick shape and slide onto a plate.
3. Reheat baguette for 2 minutes, slice and spread with soft butter. Cool raised off the surface to avoid trapped moisture.
4. Plate the eggs and bread with cucumber and nuts.

**Missing details**: Salt, milk or other egg additions are not established. A flowing center is not presented as verified fully cooked.
