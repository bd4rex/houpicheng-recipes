## 1 Thai style tomato cucumber and shrimp salad

Time reference: 00:00–00:50

**Ingredients**: Shrimp, cucumber, tomato, Thai sweet-sour chili sauce, fish sauce, garlic, small chilies and coriander roots, stems and leaves.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Boil shrimp for about 2–3 minutes. Fish sauce is described as roughly 5–8 light shakes, not a defined volume.

1. Cook shrimp, then cool in water and drain; the demonstration states about 2–3 minutes cooking.
2. Peel and core cucumber, remove the wetter tomato centers and cut both.
3. Mix with Thai sauce, a little fish sauce, garlic, chilies and cleaned coriander roots and stems.
4. Add coriander leaves last, mix and serve.

**Missing details**: Shrimp size, sauce ratios and the cooling-water conditions are unspecified. Bottle shakes cannot be converted into a fixed milliliter amount.

## 2 Curry fried rice with chicken shrimp and egg

Time reference: 00:51–01:27；01:34–02:03

**Ingredients**: Cooked rice, diced chicken breast, shrimp, previously scrambled eggs, curry powder, scallions and a little oil. Earlier meat seasoning is unknown.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: A small piece of chicken breast is used without a stated weight. Other amounts are absent.

1. Cook diced chicken and shrimp in a little oil.
2. Add thawed, loosened cooked rice, toss and season with curry powder.
3. Mix in the already scrambled eggs and a generous handful of scallions, heat through and serve.

**Missing details**: Chicken marinade, additional salt or soy sauce, curry amount and cooking time are unspecified.
