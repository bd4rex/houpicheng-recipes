## 1 Squid and crisp lotus root with XO sauce

Time reference: 00:48–01:38

**Ingredients**: Squid, lotus root, garlic, green garlic, XO sauce, a little soy sauce and oil.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Amounts, blanching time and stir-frying time are unspecified.

1. Clean the squid head, remove the eyes and other unwanted parts, wash and cut. Cut lotus root into batons.
2. Briefly blanch and drain the squid; separately blanch the lotus root.
3. Fry garlic in a little oil, add squid and cook until lightly yellowed, then add lotus root.
4. Add chopped green garlic, XO sauce and a little soy sauce. The creator suggests seafood sauce or chu hou sauce if XO sauce is unavailable.

## 2 Shiso beef cubes and peppers for a family meal

Time reference: 01:40–02:41

**Ingredients**: Diaolong beef, black pepper salt, soy sauce, oil, sugar, baking soda, starch, water, peppers, onion, garlic and shiso leaves.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Cut beef about 1 cm thick before cubing. This batch is marinated in the morning for dinner. Exact hours and seasoning proportions are unstated.

1. Cube beef and mix with black pepper salt, soy sauce, oil, sugar, a little baking soda, starch and water. Refrigerate when preparing well in advance.
2. Spread beef in a lightly oiled pan and brown one side before turning.
3. Add peppers, onion and garlic, then shiso near the end and stir fry until finished.
