## 1 Cantonese beef brisket with peanut and chu hou sauces

Time reference: 00:00–00:54；01:04–01:10

**Ingredients**: Beef brisket or rib strips, chu hou sauce, peanut butter, soy sauce, ginger and strained beef cooking liquid.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: 2 spoons chu hou sauce, a small lump of peanut butter, about 2 circles of soy sauce and 1 piece of ginger. Pressure cook on low for 20 minutes after steam appears.

1. Cut and blanch the beef. The original cooking liquid is strained and retained.
2. Return beef to the pot with chu hou sauce, peanut butter, soy sauce, retained liquid and ginger. No extra salt is added in this demonstration.
3. Close the pressure cooker and reduce to low after steam appears. Cook 20 minutes and depressurize according to the appliance before opening.

## 3 Pan seared rice rolls with beef brisket

Time reference: 01:20–01:53

**Ingredients**: Rolled rice noodles, soy sauce, a little oil, this video's cooked brisket and cooked greens. The Cantonese name does not mean pork intestines are used.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Amounts and frying time are unspecified.

1. Cut the rice rolls and mix with a little soy sauce for color and flavor.
2. Spread in a lightly oiled pan and sear, turning occasionally until lightly browned.
3. Add hot brisket, a little braising sauce and cooked greens, heat through and serve.
