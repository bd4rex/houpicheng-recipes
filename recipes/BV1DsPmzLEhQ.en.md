## 2 Butter-toasted bread with avocado and fried eggs

Time reference: 01:00–01:39；01:44–02:07

**Ingredients**: Bread, butter, eggs and avocado, served with small tomatoes and the berries visible on screen.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Two eggs are shown and the finished breakfast is described as two portions. Butter, avocado and cooking time are not quantified.

1. Warm the bread in a frying pan, turn it and add a little butter to continue toasting.
2. Crack two eggs into the same pan and cover to cook; the creator says this preserves their surface appearance.
3. Mash avocado, spread it on the toasted bread and top with the fried eggs.
4. Divide into two portions and plate with small tomatoes and berries.
