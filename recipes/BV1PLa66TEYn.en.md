## 1 Steamed egg custard with dried scallop shreds

Time reference: 00:00–00:19；00:42–00:47；02:44–02:49

**Ingredients**: Eggs, warm water, salt and soaked shredded dried scallops. A little dark finishing sauce is visible but unnamed.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Egg-to-water volume ratio 1:2; steam over medium heat for 7 minutes, then rest covered 2–3 minutes.

1. Mix eggs with twice their volume of warm water and a little salt.
2. Pour into a plate, add some scallop shreds and cover the plate before covering the steamer.
3. Steam over medium heat 7 minutes and rest 2–3 minutes. Serve with the small amount of dark sauce shown.

**Missing details**: Egg count, scallop amount and soaking time, exact water temperature and sauce identity are unspecified.

## 2 Steamed luffa with garlic and dried scallops

Time reference: 00:20–00:42；00:48–01:18

**Ingredients**: Luffa, finely minced garlic, starch, peanut oil, salt and dried scallop shreds.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Steam for 5 minutes. Other amounts are unspecified.

1. Peel and cut the luffa and mince garlic finely.
2. Immediately before steaming, mix with garlic, starch, peanut oil and salt to avoid drawing out water too early.
3. Plate, scatter scallop shreds and steam 5 minutes.

## 3 Blanched squid and vegetables with scallion soy dressing

Time reference: 01:20–02:18；02:51–02:56

**Ingredients**: Squid, green beans, thin carrot slices, okra and water; scallions, oil, soy sauce and sugar for the dressing. Wasabi is optional.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Separate boiling times and dressing ratios are unspecified.

1. Cook beans and thin carrot slices, then cook okra and plate the vegetables.
2. Separately cook the cut squid and arrange over the vegetables.
3. Fry scallions in oil until fragrant, remove the scallions and turn off the heat. Mix soy sauce and sugar into the oil.
4. Pour over squid and vegetables. The creator also suggests wasabi.

**Missing details**: Oil temperature, bean and squid cooking durations, salinity and sugar quantity are unknown.
