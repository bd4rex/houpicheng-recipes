## 1 Black pepper pork cubes without added frying oil

Time reference: 00:00–00:39

**Ingredients**: Guifei pork, also called rib meat or second-layer pork by the creator, onion, green pepper and black pepper salt.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Cut into dice-like cubes. Meat weight, seasoning amount and cooking time are unspecified.

1. Cube the pork and put it directly in the pan without additional oil. Render its own fat and brown the meat.
2. Add onion and green pepper, stir fry, then season with black pepper salt and finish cooking.

## 2 Spinach stir fried with sliced garlic

Time reference: 00:52–01:18

**Ingredients**: Spinach, sliced garlic, a little oil, salt, a little MSG and water.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Amounts and times are not given.

1. Blanch and drain the spinach.
2. Fry sliced garlic in a little oil, add spinach and stir fry.
3. Add salt, a little MSG and a little water to dissolve the seasoning. The video retains cleaned tender roots.

**Missing details**: Blanching time and salt-oil quantities are unknown. Any retained roots must first be cleaned.
