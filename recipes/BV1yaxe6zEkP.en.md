## 1 White pepper beef soup

Time reference: 00:00–00:41；01:47–02:25

**Ingredients**: Beef shank, white peppercorns, chopped celery, salt and water.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: About one bowl of water per person; pressure-cook for about 10 minutes. Bowl capacity, meat weight and pepper quantity are unspecified.

1. Dry-toast the peppercorns without oil, cool them, then crush them in a bag.
2. Slice and blanch the beef, rinse away the foam and transfer it to a pressure cooker.
3. Add the crushed pepper and roughly one bowl of water per serving; pressure-cook for about 10 minutes.
4. Put celery and salt in serving bowls, then ladle in the beef and hot soup.
