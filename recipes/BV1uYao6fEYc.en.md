## 1 Gently poached chicken with shallot ginger dip

Time reference: 00:00–00:24；00:43–00:50；01:10–01:17；01:33–01:40；01:59–02:13

**Ingredients**: Prepared whole chicken, water and salt; shallots, sand ginger, garlic or grated ginger, peanut oil and soy sauce for the dip.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Maintain the lowest heat with a gentle simmer and poach about 30 minutes. Chicken weight and water volume are absent.

1. Put the whole chicken in hot water and add a little salt. Pork belly shares the pot in this video.
2. Keep the water gently simmering on the lowest heat for about 30 minutes, then remove the chicken.
3. Cool and chop. An ice-water bath is suggested, but the full bath is not shown.
4. Chop shallot, sand ginger and garlic and mix with peanut oil and soy sauce. Grated ginger is offered as an alternative.

**Missing details**: Chicken weight, initial temperature and internal doneness are unknown, so 30 minutes cannot be generalized to every chicken. Dip ratios are absent.

## 3 Prepared beef offal simmered with radish

Time reference: 00:34–00:42；01:07–01:10；01:51–01:58

**Ingredients**: White radish, previously prepared beef offal and its sauce, with coriander visible at the end.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: The radish has been scalded beforehand. Subsequent simmering time is unknown.

1. Cut and briefly scald the radish.
2. Put it in a clay pot with the prepared offal and sauce, cover and simmer until the radish is cooked.
3. Finish with a little coriander as shown.

**Missing details**: The offal's original braising recipe, additional water, radish quantity and total time are unspecified.

## 4 Plain boiled pork belly

Time reference: 00:00–00:19；01:14–01:22；02:05–02:13

**Ingredients**: Pork belly, water and salt, served with this video's shallot-ginger-garlic oil and soy dip.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Pork weight, thickness and its separate cooking time are not given.

1. Place pork belly in the pot first and cook alongside the chicken until done.

## 5 Air fried previously boiled pork belly

Time reference: 00:51–00:55；01:22–01:32；01:40–01:44

**Ingredients**: Previously boiled pork belly and prepared barbecue seasoning.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Air-fryer temperature and duration are not supplied.

1. Cut off a piece of the already cooked pork belly and air fry it.
2. Brown the surface and render some fat, then remove and cut into pieces.
3. Sprinkle with a little prepared barbecue seasoning.
