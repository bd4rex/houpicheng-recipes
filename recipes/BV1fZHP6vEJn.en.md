## 1 Fish sauce stir fried cuttlefish

Time reference: 00:05–00:35；01:00–01:43

**Ingredients**: Cuttlefish, shallots, garlic, fish sauce, pepper, oil, a little soy sauce, celery, peppers and onion. Vegetables are identified visually.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Weights, marinating time and seasoning ratios are unspecified. No extra salt is added to the marinade.

1. Slice the cuttlefish thinly, alternating complete and partial cuts when keeping larger pieces. Lightly crush shallots and garlic.
2. Mix with fish sauce and pepper. The creator omits extra salt because the fish sauce is salty.
3. Heat and oil the pan, spread out the cuttlefish and aromatics and let one side brown before turning.
4. Add celery, peppers and onion, then a little soy sauce; cook and plate.

## 3 Watercress soup with dace paste and fish puffs
