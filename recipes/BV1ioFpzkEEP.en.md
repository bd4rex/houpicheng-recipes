## 1 Almonds coated with salted butter chocolate

Time reference: 00:10–00:20；01:03–01:29

**Ingredients**: Chocolate described by the creator as sugar-free, butter, a little salt and almonds.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: One piece of butter and a little salt are mentioned. Chocolate/almond weights, water-bath temperature and setting time are absent.

1. Put the chocolate in a container with a piece of butter and a little salt.
2. Stand the container in hot water to melt the mixture and stir.
3. Coat the almonds; place some individually on the white tray and spread another portion in a bag.
4. Refrigerate or freeze until set, then lift off the individual pieces and slice the bagged portion. No duration is given.

## 2 Almonds with honey syrup butter and chocolate

Time reference: 00:20–01:02；01:30–02:03

**Ingredients**: White sugar, salt, butter, chocolate, honey and almonds.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: No quantities are supplied. Turning off the heat when large bubbles appear is explicitly shown.

1. Heat the white sugar and salt in a pan until the large-bubble stage shown, then turn off the heat.
2. Stir in butter, chocolate and honey using the residual heat.
3. Mix in almonds and spread into a paper-lined container.
4. Leave to set and divide into small pieces; the creator calls this version sweeter.
