## 1 Roasted salmon peppers and potatoes with shrimp and egg

Time reference: 00:00–00:18；00:59–02:58

**Ingredients**: Salmon, potatoes, red and yellow sweet peppers, green pepper, shrimp, egg, lemon zest and juice, olive oil, black pepper salt and an unidentified dried herb.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Microwave potatoes without added water on high for 3 minutes. Air fry at 180°C for 15 minutes. One egg is fried; other weights and microwave wattage are unspecified.

1. Scrub the potatoes, retain the skins and cut into pieces. Microwave without added water on high for 3 minutes, leaving them partly cooked.
2. Remove the sweet pepper seeds. Arrange peppers, potatoes and salmon on the tray, with the fish in a small dish. Air fry at 180°C for 15 minutes.
3. Separately pan fry the shrimp and egg until cooked.
4. Remove charred pepper skins and cut the peppers into pieces. Plate with potatoes, salmon, shrimp and egg.
5. Add lemon zest and juice, a little olive oil and black pepper salt. The video also adds a small amount of an unidentified dried herb.
