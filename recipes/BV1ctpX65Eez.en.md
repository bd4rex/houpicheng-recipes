## 1 Pan toasted shrimp egg and peanut butter sandwich

Time reference: 00:00–00:03；00:20–01:18

**Ingredients**: Shrimp paste, egg, sliced bread, peanut butter, butter and a little oil.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: 1 bread slice cut in half and 1 egg. Shrimp-paste amount, oil and cooking time are unspecified.

1. Thaw the shrimp paste until soft and pan fry it, with an egg beside it.
2. Place the two bread halves over the egg so they adhere, then turn.
3. Spread peanut butter, add a little butter to crisp the bread, add the cooked shrimp patty and close with gentle pressure.
4. Toast both sides until golden. Rest the sandwich raised on cutlery to reduce moisture underneath.

**Missing details**: Shrimp-paste formula, bread thickness, heat and internal doneness parameters are absent. Suggested cheese is not treated as actually added.

## 3 Cantonese beef brisket udon with greens

Time reference: 02:15–02:54

**Ingredients**: Frozen udon, greens, prepared Cantonese beef brisket and its sauce, and water.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: The udon used cooks for about 1 minute. Beef and liquid quantities are unspecified.
