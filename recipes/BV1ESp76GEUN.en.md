## 3 Rice with curried beef potato and spinach

Time reference: 01:03–01:28；01:40–02:27

**Ingredients**: Minced beef, potatoes, carrot, garlic, salt, a little curry powder, coriander, scallions, spinach, oil and cooked rice.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Microwave potato and carrot pieces without water on high for 3 minutes. Cut carrots smaller than potatoes. Other amounts are unknown.

1. Mince the beef. Dice potatoes and carrots and microwave without water for 3 minutes on high until partly cooked.
2. Fry garlic in a little oil, add beef and break it up, then add the vegetables and fry until the potatoes lightly brown.
3. Season with salt and a little curry powder, add coriander, scallions and chopped spinach, and cook through.
4. Mix into cooked rice and portion.

**Missing details**: Beef weight, curry quantity, microwave wattage and whether spinach was pre-blanched are unstated. The visible coriander is not replaced by the ASR's mistaken lettuce.
