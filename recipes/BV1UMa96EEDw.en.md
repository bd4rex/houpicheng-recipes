## 1 Shrimp egg and cream cheese sandwich

Time reference: 00:00–00:22；00:31–02:02

**Ingredients**: Shrimp paste, egg, wholemeal sliced bread, cream cheese, tomato, cucumber, water and a little oil for the shrimp patty.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: One egg and two bread slices are visible. Egg and toast cooking times cannot be reliably read.

1. Crack the egg into a pan, add a little water and cover to cook without oil.
2. Thaw the shrimp paste and pan fry in a little oil. Let one side set before flattening, turning and cooking through.
3. Toast the bread and cool it raised off the surface, then spread cream cheese. Remove the watery cucumber center and shave thinly; slice tomato.
4. Fill the bread with shrimp, tomato, egg and cucumber, wrap tightly and cut in half.
