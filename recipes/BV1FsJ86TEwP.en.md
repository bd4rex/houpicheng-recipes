## 1 Sweet and sour ribs and chicken wings with preserved plums

Time reference: 00:05–00:31；01:28–01:31；02:00–02:05

**Confirmed amounts and times**: 2 preserved plums. Rib, wing, seasoning and broth amounts are not supplied.

1. Transfer the cooked ribs to a small pot and add several chicken wings.
2. Add sugar, vinegar, soy sauce, 2 preserved plums and a little rib broth.
