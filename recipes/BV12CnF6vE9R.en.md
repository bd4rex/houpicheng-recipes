## 2 Braised beef egg potato and vegetable plate

Time reference: 00:20–00:55；01:16–03:11

**Ingredients**: Prepared braised beef, potatoes, eggs, green beans, shiitake, broccoli or broccolini, nuts and black pepper salt. Chili powder and soy sauce are optional suggestions.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Start potatoes first and add eggs for the final 10 minutes. This personal plate uses roughly a fist-sized portion of beef and 1 potato; other amounts are unspecified.

1. Boil potatoes first. Warm the eggs briefly with some warm water before adding them to cook for about 10 minutes.
2. Cool and peel the eggs. Separately boil the beans, shiitake and broccoli-type greens until cooked.
3. Slice the prepared beef thickly and plate with the vegetables, eggs, peeled potato and a few nuts.
4. Season with black pepper salt; chili powder or soy sauce are suggested alternatives.

**Missing details**: Total potato time, the time needed to cook the beans through and full portion sizes are absent. This individual plate is not a universal weight-loss target.
