## 1 Chicken drumsticks with wuzhi maotao

Time reference: 00:05–00:39；01:31–01:43

**Ingredients**: Chicken drumsticks, salt-baked-chicken seasoning, peanut oil, wuzhi maotao, scallions and ginger slices. Ginger is visible on the tray.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: About half a packet of seasoning; marinate about one hour. Air-fry at 180°C for about 20 minutes, turning partway through. Packet weight and chicken weight are unclear.

1. Score the drumsticks twice, coat with roughly half a packet of seasoning and a little peanut oil, and marinate about an hour.
2. Soak the wuzhi maotao briefly and place it with scallions and ginger under the chicken.
3. Air-fry at 180°C for about 20 minutes, turning partway through to brown both sides.
4. Pan-frying followed by clay-pot cooking is mentioned as an alternative, but air-frying is the demonstrated method.

## 2 Tomato tofu with minced pork

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Weights and seasoning ratios are unspecified.

## 4 Boiled beans and broccoli with shallot garlic dipping sauce

Time reference: 01:10–01:17；01:44–01:51

**Ingredients**: Beans, broccoli, shallots, garlic, peanut oil, soy sauce and water.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Boiling time, oil temperature and seasoning amounts are unspecified.

1. Boil the beans first, add broccoli, cook through and drain.
2. Finely chop shallots and garlic and combine in a small dish with peanut oil and soy sauce.
3. Serve the vegetables with the dip. Beans need to be cooked through; do not reuse a two-minute timing from another vegetable.
