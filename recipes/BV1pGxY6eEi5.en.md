## 1 Fish maw milk jelly with dates longan and ginger

Time reference: 00:00–00:44；01:48–02:14

**Ingredients**: Soaked fish maw, grated ginger, pitted red dates, dried longan, goji berries, milk and water.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: On-screen text says to steam dried fish maw with ginger for about 15 minutes, then soak it in the refrigerator for one day. Main stewing time and the fish-maw-to-milk ratio are unspecified.

1. Steam and refrigerate-soak the fish maw, open its folds, remove trapped blood and cut it into small pieces.
2. Stew some fish maw with generous grated ginger, pitted dates, longan and water.
3. With about 10 minutes remaining, add goji berries and milk and continue cooking. No extra sugar is added in this demonstration.
4. Portion and refrigerate until jelly-like. The remaining soaked fish maw is separately frozen for later meals.

## 2 Soybean brown rice portions

Time reference: 00:53–01:22；02:16–02:47

## 3 Soybeans in tomato sauce

Time reference: 01:22–01:46；02:48–03:07

**Ingredients**: Soaked soybeans, tomato paste, water, salt, a little sugar and a sprig of an unidentified herb.

3. After releasing pressure appropriately, check tenderness; the demonstrated beans crush easily between the fingers.
