## 1 Ginger pork patty toast sandwich

Time reference: 00:00–00:52；00:55–00:59；01:43–02:10

**Ingredients**: Minced pork shoulder, salt, grated ginger, water, a little baking soda, cornstarch, oil, sliced bread and peanut butter.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Two small patties are fried. The toaster dial is around 3.5–4; this is a setting, not an explicit number of minutes. Other amounts are unstated.

1. Mix pork with salt, grated ginger and a little water, then baking soda and cornstarch. Stir and slap the mixture until sticky.
2. Place two small portions in a lightly oiled pan. Turn and flatten after the bottoms set, and cook both sides.
3. Toast the bread, spread with peanut butter, add the patties, close and cut.
