## 3 Scallion shrimp

Time reference: 01:19–01:38.

**Ingredients:** Shrimp pieces already coated with a white powder, scallions, oil and a red squeeze-bottle sauce. Powder and sauce identities and all quantities are unspecified.

1. Place the coated shrimp in the pan individually, fry until their color changes and the surface browns, then remove them.
2. Fry the scallions in oil, return the shrimp and toss.
3. Squeeze in the red sauce, toss and plate. This final addition is visible around 01:30–01:32.

**Key point:** The creator describes the finished dish as sweet and sour. The frying and scallion-return sequence is visible.
