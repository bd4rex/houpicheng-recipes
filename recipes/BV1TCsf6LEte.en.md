## 1 Beef and Chinese sausage rice

Time reference: 00:20–02:10；02:52–02:59

**Ingredients**: Simiao rice, water, minced beef, Chinese sausage, egg, cooked greens, scallions, ginger and garlic. Beef marinade: oyster sauce, light soy sauce, salt, oil, sugar and starch. Finishing sauce: soy sauce, hot water and sugar.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: Soak rice for about 30 minutes; initially cook it for about five minutes and cook about five more minutes after adding meat. Rice-to-water ratio and quantities are unspecified.

1. Soak the rice until a grain breaks easily. Season the minced beef with the stated marinade and ginger and garlic.
2. Put rice, water and a little oil in the pan; cover and cook for about five minutes until bubbling and the water is receding.
3. Spread the beef and sliced sausage evenly, cover and cook for about five more minutes. Add the egg partway through; the creator suggests adding it with the meat for a fully cooked egg.
4. Add cooked greens and scallions. Pour on the soy sauce, hot-water and sugar mixture just before eating.
5. For a clay pot, the creator separately suggests alternating two to three minutes of heating with two to three minutes covered off the heat, repeated two or three times, to form a crust.

**Missing details**: Rice, water, beef and sausage amounts are not given. The clay-pot advice is kept separate from the demonstrated frying-pan method.

## 2 Black pepper beef with celery

Time reference: 00:03–00:19；02:11–02:50

**Ingredients**: Beef, celery, peppers, onion, black pepper, salt, soy sauce, starch, a little baking soda, sugar, oil, water and black pepper sauce. Vegetables are confirmed visually.

**Confirmed amounts and times**: One spoonful of water is added to the marinade; other quantities, marinating duration and heat are unspecified.

1. Cut the beef fairly thick and mix with pepper, salt, soy sauce, starch, a little baking soda, one spoonful of water, sugar and oil.
2. Sear one side before moving or turning the meat.
3. Add celery, peppers and onion, then a little black pepper sauce.
4. The creator says thick pieces can be briefly covered if not yet cooked through.

**Missing details**: Beef cut, weight, thickness and seasoning ratios are unclear. Baking soda is described only as a small amount.
